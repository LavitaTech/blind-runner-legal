Blind Runner – Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 7, 2026

Blind Runner (“the App”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the App.

1. Information We Collect

Blind Runner does not collect personal information directly from users. The App may use Apple services such as Game Center to support features like leaderboards and achievements. Apple manages Game Center data under its own policies.

Game Center & Leaderboards: If you choose to enable global leaderboards, the App submits your gameplay scores to Apple Game Center so they can be ranked on leaderboards. Your Game Center nickname and score may be visible to other players on global rankings. You can disable global leaderboards at any time in the App (Info Center → Privacy & Legal) and the App will stop submitting new scores.

The App may also include third-party advertising in future versions. If enabled, advertising partners may collect limited information to provide and measure ads (for example, device identifiers, approximate location, and usage or interaction data). Any information collected by third-party ad providers is governed by their own privacy policies.

2. Data We Do Not Collect

Blind Runner does not:

  • Sell user data
  • Collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or contacts
  • Collect precise location data
  • Include in-app messaging or chat features
  • Access photos, camera, microphone, or files

Note: If third-party ads are enabled in a future version, ad partners may collect certain device-level information (such as an advertising identifier or app interaction data) to deliver and measure ads. Blind Runner does not receive or store your personal contact information (such as name, email, phone number, or contacts) for advertising purposes.

3. Analytics

Blind Runner may use basic, privacy-friendly analytics (such as Apple-provided analytics) to understand stability and improve gameplay performance. If third-party advertising is enabled in the future, the advertising SDK may also provide aggregated reporting or measurement related to ad performance.

Blind Runner does not use analytics to sell user data. Where required, the App will request consent in accordance with applicable laws and platform requirements (including Apple’s AppTrackingTransparency framework, if applicable).

4. Advertising and Tracking

Blind Runner may add third-party ads in a future release. Depending on the advertising provider and your device settings, ads may be contextual or personalized. If an ad provider uses data for personalized advertising or measurement that qualifies as “tracking” under Apple policies, the App will prompt you for permission where required.

5. Children’s Privacy

Blind Runner does not knowingly collect personal data from children. Any data associated with Game Center is handled by Apple. If advertising is enabled in the future, we will use reasonable efforts to configure ad services in a way that is appropriate for the App’s audience.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the integrity of the App. Because Blind Runner does not store personal user data on external servers, risk exposure is minimal.

7. Third-Party Services

Blind Runner relies on Apple Game Center (leaderboards and achievements). Use of Apple services is governed by Apple’s policies. If advertising is enabled in the future, the App may also use third-party ad networks and their associated services, which are governed by their respective privacy policies.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
